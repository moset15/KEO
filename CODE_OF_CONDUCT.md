# Code of conduct

KEO welcomes contributors who want public evidence to be easier to inspect. Treat people with respect, make technical disagreement specific and constructive, and distinguish a documented fact from an inference. Political viewpoint is not a reason to exclude someone from a factual discussion.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualised conduct, impersonation and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces to organise political targeting, profile voters or make unsupported accusations against people or groups. Keep examples focused on the technical or evidence question and remove unnecessary personal details.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and spaces where someone represents KEO. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or block accounts according to the conduct and its impact. Decisions should explain the relevant behaviour while protecting confidential reporting details.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through an available private contact channel. If no channel is listed, request one without publicly naming affected people or reproducing abusive material. This MVP has no dedicated conduct-reporting address or guaranteed response time. Security disclosures follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
