# Product Hunt demo and release checklist

Target: 18 September 2026. Public launch is an owner-approved release, not an automatic consequence of committing code.

## Two-minute demo

1. Open Ask and choose the constitutional scheduling example. Inspect the primary source, publication/retrieval dates and assessment.
2. Enter a new claim about an election notice. Show that curated context cannot authenticate it and that missing evidence stays explicit.
3. Open Verify, preview/redact an image and read its text on-device. Correct it, confirm the text review and check sources. Show that no pixels or model calls are involved; manual entry remains available.
4. Browse a reference threat, then the county map. Point out hypothetical examples and illustrative records, not live incidents.
5. Open Methodology: evidence rules, privacy, no-paid-AI policy and the roadmap from the lightweight Site to the full 3D application.

Describe KEO as an evidence navigator, not an omniscient fact checker. Do not claim a live provider, real-time feed, independent incident verification or universal device support.

## Before public release

- Review curated sources, attribution, licences and dates.
- Pass type/build/unit and mobile/browser checks; inspect keyboard and enlarged-text access.
- Record throttled mobile performance and real-device gaps.
- Review the security report and confirm no secrets in code/history.
- Validate the native hosted preview, storage/delete behaviour and unavailable states.
- Keep AI disabled with no budget. Enabling it requires provider tests, consent/privacy review and spending controls.
- Review Sites account limits and public access settings; no assumed traffic capacity or domain entitlement.
- Push reviewed slices and retain a rollback checkpoint.
- Obtain owner approval to publish publicly.

After launch, prioritise observed user needs and evidence coverage before starting the shared core or 3D workspace. No result declaration, voter targeting or citizen report publication without appropriate safeguards.
