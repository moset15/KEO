# Confidence

Confidence describes confidence in the **assessment**, not the probability that a person or group is malicious. It is not a calibrated statistical probability or a score for a voter, organisation or community.

| Label   | Interpretation                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| High    | Direct applicable primary evidence or strong independent corroboration supports the assessment, with limited material uncertainty |
| Medium  | Credible relevant evidence exists, but a material uncertainty remains                                                             |
| Low     | Evidence is limited, indirect or conflicting                                                                                      |
| Unknown | Evidence is insufficient to justify a confidence judgement                                                                        |

Explain the reason for confidence through the cited evidence and gaps. Count independence, relevance and freshness, not just links. A primary source can be authoritative about its own announcement while being insufficient to establish an unrelated claim. Confident language from a model is not evidence.

The live adapter applies basic safeguards, including rejecting invented evidence IDs and forcing insufficient evidence/unknown when no evidence is cited. These checks do not guarantee that every accepted confidence label is well judged. The underlying sources remain available for review. Curated background and provider failure must not be presented as a high-confidence current verification.
